## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the Smart Budget Tracker website or open the application
2. Click on "Register" or "Sign Up"
3. Enter your name, email address, and password
4. Click "Create Account"
5. Verify your email address by clicking the link in the verification email

### Logging In

1. Visit the Smart Budget Tracker website or open the application
2. Enter your email address and password
3. Click "Log In"
4. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, enter the verification code

### Setting Up Your Profile

1. Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
3. Complete your profile information
4. Set your preferred currency, language, and theme
5. Click "Save Changes"

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of your financial situation at a glance.

### Key Features

- **Financial Summary**: View your total income, expenses, and net income
- **Budget Status**: See your budget progress for the current month
- **Recent Transactions**: View your most recent income and expenses
- **Spending by Category**: See a breakdown of your spending by category
- **Monthly Spending Trend**: Track your spending trends over time

### Customizing Your Dashboard

1. Click on the "Customize" button in the top-right corner of the dashboard
2. Drag and drop widgets to rearrange them
3. Click on the gear icon on each widget to configure it
4. Click "Save Layout" when you're done

## Budget Management

Budgets help you plan and control your spending in different categories.

### Creating a Budget

1. Go to the "Budgets" page
2. Click "Create Budget"
3. Enter a name for your budget
4. Set the budget amount
5. Select a category (optional)
6. Set the start and end dates
7. Add notes (optional)
8. Click "Create Budget"

### Editing a Budget

1. Go to the "Budgets" page
2. Find the budget you want to edit
3. Click on the three dots (⋮) and select "Edit"
4. Make your changes
5. Click "Save Changes"

### Deleting a Budget

1. Go to the "Budgets" page
2. Find the budget you want to delete
3. Click on the three dots (⋮) and select "Delete"
4. Confirm the deletion

### Sharing a Budget

1. Go to the "Budgets" page
2. Find the budget you want to share
3. Click on the three dots (⋮) and select "Share"
4. Enter the email address of the person you want to share with
5. Select their permission level (view or edit)
6. Click "Share Budget"

## Expense Tracking

Track your income and expenses to keep a record of your financial transactions.

### Adding an Expense

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Click "Add Expense"
3. Enter the expense details:
   - Description
   - Amount
   - Date
   - Category
   - Budget (optional)
   - Payment method (optional)
   - Location (optional)
   - Notes (optional)
   - Tags (optional)
4. Upload a receipt (optional)
5. Click "Save Expense"

### Adding Income

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Click "Add Income"
3. Enter the income details:
   - Description
   - Amount
   - Date
   - Category
   - Notes (optional)
   - Tags (optional)
4. Click "Save Income"

### Editing a Transaction

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Find the transaction you want to edit
3. Click on the three dots (⋮) and select "Edit"
4. Make your changes
5. Click "Save Changes"

### Deleting a Transaction

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Find the transaction you want to delete
3. Click on the three dots (⋮) and select "Delete"
4. Confirm the deletion

### Filtering and Searching Transactions

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Use the search bar to search for specific transactions
3. Use the filters to narrow down transactions by:
   - Date range
   - Category
   - Type (income or expense)
   - Amount range
   - Tags
   - Payment method
4. Click "Apply Filters"

### Importing Transactions

1. Go to the "Expenses" page
2. Click "Import"
3. Select the file format (CSV, Excel, etc.)
4. Upload your file
5. Map the columns to the appropriate fields
6. Click "Import Transactions"

## Bank Account Integration

Connect your bank accounts to automatically import transactions.

### Connecting a Bank Account

1. Go to the "Settings" page
2. Click on "Bank Accounts"
3. Click "Connect Account"
4. Select your bank from the list
5. Log in to your bank account

1. Go to the "Expenses" page

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Login Issues

- **Forgot Password**: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions
- **Account Locked**: Contact support if your account is locked after multiple failed login attempts
- **Two-Factor Authentication Issues**: Use the recovery codes provided when you set up 2FA

#### Transaction Import Issues

- **Connection Failed**: Try reconnecting your bank account
- **Duplicate Transactions**: Mark duplicates and they will be merged automatically
- **Missing Transactions**: Check the date range for the import

#### Performance Issues

- **Slow Loading**: Try clearing your browser cache
- **App Crashes**: Update to the latest version of the application
- **Data Not Syncing**: Check your internet connection
